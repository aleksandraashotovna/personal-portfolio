About

I’m a Product Design Lead, who combines hands-on work with mentoring and process management. For the last decade, I've mainly been working on complex systems, but I also enjoy tackling smaller projects that allow for more creativity and experimentation.

My biggest passion is building scalable processes and systems that allow teams, small or big, to develop complex products efficiently, consistently and without reliance on a single person. Among my professional interests are design systems, accessibility and web development.
